This playbook provides **comprehensive 1:1 Chinese and English parity across 10 major industries**. Every industry profile establishes:
1. **`### A. 小字清理与真角标保护边界 (Micro-Copy Boundary & Protected Footnotes)`**: 8-10 banned AI pill kickers and redundant micro-copies vs. explicit statutory/regulatory standard codes (ISO, GB/T, IEC, FDA, NMPA, GAAP, SEC, Scope 1-3, IEEE, arXiv) with 4-6 authentic bilingual footnote examples.
2. **`### B. 伪黑话黑名单 vs. 真专业白名单 (Slop vs. Domain Terms)`**: Structured markdown table with 15-20 banned slop terms and 20-25 authentic domain terms per language (both ZH and EN).
3. **`### C. 中英 1:1 改写范例 (Before → After)`**: At least 2 distinct pairs of Before (AI Slop) -> After (Human Pro) per language (Chinese and English), totaling >= 20 ZH pairs and >= 20 EN pairs across the repository.
4. **`### D. 中英高质感表达模版与高说服力句式 (Sentence Patterns & Rewrite Templates)`**: At least 4-5 high-impact sentence patterns and templates per language per industry, totaling >= 40 ZH templates and >= 40 EN templates.
